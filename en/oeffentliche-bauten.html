Focus

Public buildings for municipalities

apb architekten ag from Uster has completed 11 public buildings since 2010 – in Fällanden, Gossau, Grüningen, Herrliberg, Maur, Schwerzenbach, Uster, Volketswil, Wald and Wädenswil. 8 of them after winning a competition or consultant selection (1st place).

Clients: Catholic Parish of Bruder Klaus; City of Uster; Municipality of Gossau; Municipality of Grüningen; Municipality of Herrliberg; Municipality of Maur; Municipality of Wald; Roman Catholic Parish of Dübendorf; SBB AG (Swiss Federal Railways); Stiftung Kinderhospiz Schweiz (Swiss Children’s Hospice Foundation).
