Apprenticeship training

Apprenticeship
from August 2028

Every two years our practice trains an architectural draughtsperson leading to the Swiss Federal VET Diploma (EFZ). The next apprenticeship starts in August 2028.

Architectural draughtsperson EFZ

Application documents by post

  1. Cover letter (must be handwritten)
  2. CV (handwritten or typed) with a photo, including a referee (e.g. your class teacher’s name and telephone number)
  3. Aptitude tests such as Stellwerk, Multicheck or similar
  4. All school reports from recent years (secondary school / grammar school)
  5. Documents from taster placements, drawing classes etc. (optional, if available)

Following a possible interview, which would take place in July, August or September 2027, we will invite you to a taster day at our office. We do not offer taster placements beforehand.

If you are a pupil and this appeals to you, we would love to get to know you. Please send your complete application by post to:
