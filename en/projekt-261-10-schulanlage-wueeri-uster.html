Wüeri School Complex Uster

Extension, conversion and refurbishment of the listed Wüeri school complex

Extension, conversion and refurbishment of the listed Wüeri school complex from 1966/1974, carried out in phases while the school remained in use.

Brief

During the conversion and refurbishment of the school complex, barrier-free access for people with disabilities was improved, rooms were swapped, and wheelchair-accessible toilets and a stairlift were installed. Individual rooms such as the natural sciences room, the staff room, the music hall and the caretaker’s flat were modernised and brought up to today’s standards. Measures for fire safety and earthquake resistance were also necessary.

Façade refurbishment

The façade was refurbished in close consultation with the cantonal heritage conservation office, as wings A and B are listed on the Canton of Zurich’s inventory of buildings worthy of protection. The external insulation, partly clad with ceramic tiles at the end of the 1980s, was removed. For energy reasons and to improve ventilation, the old windows in wings A and B were replaced with new ones.

Materials and colour

In consultation with the cantonal heritage conservation office and on the basis of colour analyses, the exposed concrete façades of wings A and B from the 1960s and 1970s and the corridors of wing A were finished and painted in keeping with their original character. The corridors also received new energy-efficient lighting.

Gymnasium

To improve ventilation in the two gymnasiums, automatic window-opening and shading systems were installed. The lower gymnasium received a barrier-free side entrance. At the same time, an accessible changing room and toilet were integrated into a covered side extension. This entrance also serves as an emergency exit for the lower gymnasium, so that the hall can now be used for events with larger numbers of people.

ICT cabling

Following a uniform concept, the entire school complex was rewired and networked internally and fitted with Wi-Fi access points.
