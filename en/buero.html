Practice

Architects.
Planners.
Site supervisors.

At apb architekten, a team of architects, site supervisors and draughtspeople has worked closely together for many years. Drawing on our complementary skills, and together with our clients, we create innovative architectural solutions that stand out for their quality, functionality, cost-effectiveness and sustainability.

Profile

2011

Today

Our practice is one of the leading names when modern architecture and reliable construction management are called for.

Main areas of work

  • Architecture
  • Site supervision / construction management

Core competencies

  • Consultancy, design and delivery of new builds and extensions
  • Listed buildings
  • MINERGIE and MINERGIE-P buildings
  • Overall management of general planning teams

Buildings

  • Residential buildings
  • Public buildings (schools, municipal buildings, care and retirement centres)
  • Buildings for Swiss Federal Railways (SBB) (regional railway stations and convenience shops)
