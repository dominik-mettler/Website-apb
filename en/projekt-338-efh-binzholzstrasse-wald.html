Project 338 · Living

Binzholzstrasse House Wald

Energy-efficiency refurbishment of a detached house

This detached house from the 1980s stands on the edge of the village of Wald in a two-storey residential zone.

Status
Completed
Completion
2022
Scope of services
SIA stages 2.1 (preliminary study) to 5.3 (commissioning)
Client
Private client

Construction period
September 2022 – December 2022, approx. 3 months

Situation

This detached house from the 1980s stands on the edge of the village of Wald in a two-storey residential zone.

Construction and materials

The existing solid building has cavity walls with little core insulation. Because of the age of the windows, blinds and external render, and to save on heating costs, an additional layer of thermal insulation was applied to the façade. To do so, the existing external timber boarding was removed from the “warm” parts of the walls. The openings were fitted with new wood-metal windows and new external venetian blinds. New colours for the rendered surfaces and the woodwork gave the house a more contemporary appearance.

Special features

The windows were replaced during the owners’ two-week holiday, without any structural alterations inside the house and without disturbance to its interior.
