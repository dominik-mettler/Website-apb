  • Apartment buildings with up to 50 units

Advice.

With more than 100 successfully completed projects in the public and private sectors, we have the expertise needed to deliver projects as well as experience in assessing properties – which benefits clients and prospective buyers alike.

The interests and requirements of public clients differ from those of private clients and investors. apb architekten offers tailored services to both.

  • Preparing study commissions and cost estimates for public building projects
  • Advice on building regulations
  • Drawing up project schedules
  • Preparing dossiers for planning and construction credit approval
  • Preparing tender documents for any further consultant selection procedures
  • Evaluating and selecting applicants
  • Help in finding suitable plots
  • Feasibility studies for new builds and conversions of existing properties
  • Building condition surveys and advice on energy-efficiency subsidies
  • Advice on building regulations
  • Cost estimates

Developers selling condominium apartments usually delegate buyer support to the building’s architect. apb architekten can guide prospective buyers step by step on your behalf.

  2. Checking feasibility

  4. Accompanying visits to showrooms

  7. Monitoring construction

Lediweg apartment building, Uster – 7 owner-occupied apartments

A survey of an existing property forms the basis for long-term planning and current building projects. apb architekten can carry out every step of the assessment of your property for you.

  • Existing drawings (floor plans, sections, elevations)
  • Land register extract
  • Latest valuation by the cantonal building insurer
  • Site inspection
  • Measured survey on site (if no existing drawings are available)
  • Transferring and drawing up plans in CAD
  • Documenting and assessing the building’s condition element by element
  • Assessing short-, medium- and long-term measures, including estimates of the budgets required
  • Report with recommended measures
  • Coordinating further surveys (earthquake safety, hazardous substances, building physics etc.)
