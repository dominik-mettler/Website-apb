Heritage protection

Oberuster Primary School was built in 1855 and gained an additional storey in 1902. It underwent extensive conversion in the 1950s. The building is on the municipal heritage inventory and was converted in line with heritage protection requirements. No significant changes could be made to its external appearance. The necessary sun protection was installed with the utmost restraint in the form of fabric blinds. The central projection on the north side was redesigned at the entrance in a simple, clear form. Inside, the parquet floors and wood panelling in the classrooms were retained to meet heritage requirements.

Brief

Until the conversion, the library and the staff room of the twelve-class school were housed provisionally in two apartments on the ground floor. Now a library meeting today’s requirements, with a book area and a media zone, has been created. In the attractively glazed anteroom, exhibitions and a play area spark the pupils’ interest. Next to the library are the head teacher’s office, a meeting room and two group rooms. The oversized classrooms of more than 90 m² were fitted with modern furnishings. Movable cupboards allow the rooms to be divided flexibly and make it possible to arrange group workspaces individually.

Controlled ventilation

The school stands on the busy Aathalstrasse. To keep noise exposure as low as possible, and for energy reasons, all teaching rooms were fitted with controlled ventilation.
