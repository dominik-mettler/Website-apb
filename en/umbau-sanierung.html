Focus

Conversion, refurbishment and extension

apb architekten ag from Uster has completed 25 conversions and refurbishments since 2002 – in Gossau, Greifensee, Grüt, Herrliberg, Hombrechtikon, Maur, Schlieren, Schwerzenbach, Uster, Volketswil, Wald, Wädenswil and Zürich-Altstetten. 12 of them after winning a competition or consultant selection (1st place).

Clients: Catholic Parish of Bruder Klaus; City of Uster; Hombrechtikon School Board; Municipality of Gossau; Municipality of Herrliberg; Municipality of Maur; Municipality of Wald; Nänikon-Greifensee School Board; SBB AG (Swiss Federal Railways); Schwerzenbach Primary School Board; Uster Primary School; Uster Secondary School Board.
