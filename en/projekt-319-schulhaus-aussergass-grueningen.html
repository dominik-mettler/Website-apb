Project 319 · Learning · Public administration

Aussergass School Grüningen

New school building in concrete-timber hybrid construction

New school building with a day nursery, kindergarten, day-care facilities, classrooms and school administration, in concrete-timber hybrid construction.

Procurement
Consultant selection, 1st place
Scope of services
SIA stages 3.1 (preliminary design) to 5.3 (commissioning)

Situation

The existing Aussergass school complex consists of several school buildings and a kindergarten, and needed an extension because of a lack of space. The site lies in the zone for public buildings and borders on the core zone and the agricultural zone.

Brief

The new building houses the school administration, classrooms, group rooms, after-school care and a kindergarten on the ground floor, allowing synergies between school and extended day care. Because of the municipality’s rapid growth, an additional storey was added just four years after the building was completed.

Appearance

The new building is set at right angles to the existing school and completes the school grounds. A link building connects it directly to the existing school and, thanks to the lift in the new building, makes all floors of the existing school accessible. The elongated building is structured by recessed entrances and loggias. A shallow hipped roof without overhang was chosen.

Construction

The load-bearing structure is a skeleton frame of concrete slabs on reinforced concrete columns with individual concrete shear walls for bracing. The rest of the building, in hybrid construction, consists of non-load-bearing plasterboard partitions and a building envelope of timber elements. The entire additional storey on the second floor and the roof are also made of prefabricated timber elements.

Materials

The façade is clad with rear-ventilated vertical timber boarding and complemented by coloured elements (blinds, horizontal metal elements). Inside, the character of the spaces is defined by rubber granulate floors, timber acoustic ceilings and exposed concrete or plastered walls.

Energy and building services

Wood chip heating. Separate ventilation systems for the kitchen and the second-floor classrooms. The building envelope meets an enhanced MINERGIE standard (without certification). A photovoltaic system was added on the roof after the additional storey was built.

Special features

The additional storey was built while the building remained in use. The roof, its superstructures, glazed elements and the ventilation systems were put into temporary storage and then reinstalled.
