Zentralstrasse Development Uster

New housing development with commercial units in the historic core zone

New development of apartment buildings in the historic core zone of Uster, with commercial units on the ground floor.

Situation

The site lies within the perimeter of the inventoried townscape of Kirchuster, which is of regional importance, and in the core zone of Uster’s historic centre, on Zentralstrasse between Brauereistrasse and the Aabach stream.

Two existing buildings were replaced and a third building was added to densify and extend the core zone. The project essentially consists of three pairs of buildings that weave themselves into their built surroundings, together form an ensemble and define new courtyards and street spaces. On the corner of Zentralstrasse and Brauereistrasse, an eaves-fronted building was replaced by one of similar size and format, but gable-fronted. Together with the neighbouring house, which is also gable-fronted, it strengthens this important urban situation and forms a new street frontage onto Zentralstrasse, which widens out at this point. Set slightly back, beside the Aabach, the farm building of the house at Zentralstrasse 17 was replaced by a residential building. Its geometry and eaves height were taken from the existing building; only the north façade now follows the course of the Aabach. The third building, on Brauereistrasse, completes the existing situation and forms a new ensemble with the existing and replaced buildings. Its large, elongated volume is comparable to the commercial buildings along the southern edge of the castle hill, but its form responds to the small scale of its immediate neighbours. With its roofs, the building, divided into two parts, picks up the grain of the surrounding development, while its differentiated geometry creates clear relationships with the existing buildings. Tapering towards the west, the building relates to the buildings along Zentralstrasse, while its north-facing gable façade defines an attractive courtyard. The façade on Brauereistrasse, set slightly back from its neighbour, anticipates the curve of the street that follows with a barely visible kink.
