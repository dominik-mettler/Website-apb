Project 329 · Learning

Aesch School Complex Maur

Extension of an existing primary school complex

Extension and conversion of a primary school complex from the 1970s with an additional wing.

Situation

Wing E is an extension of the existing Aesch school complex, which comprises several classroom wings and a gymnasium with a multi-purpose hall. The extension is placed in the southern corner of the site and connected to the other wings by a canopy.

Brief

The building houses two classrooms and two group rooms with their ancillary rooms. Outside, the playground was adapted to the new situation, and new play equipment and climbing frames were installed in the garden.

Appearance

The building is a simple cube set on the same alignment as the neighbouring wing D. There are already plans to renew wing D or add a storey to it, using the gap between the two wings as a “working joint” for the future construction work. A lift is also to be built into the gap between the buildings, so that it can be used by both parts of the building.

Construction

The building is of solid construction, with concrete floor slabs and external walls of highly insulating single-leaf masonry (49 cm). Concrete walls with XPS insulation were used only for earthquake resistance and for the window parapets in contact with the ground on the hillside.

Materials

The single-leaf masonry is rendered inside and out and matches the existing ensemble in texture and colour. Restrained natural colours for the fire doors, cloakrooms in solid oak and calming shades of green complete the look.

Energy and building services

The building is connected via a district heating pipe to the heating network of the school complex and neighbouring properties, whose plant is fired with wood chips. All rooms have controlled comfort ventilation. A large photovoltaic system is installed on the roof.

Special features

The building is MINERGIE-P certified, with the registration number “ZH-769-P”.
