SBB Railway Station & Shop Bonstetten

New timber building to MINERGIE level with pellet heating

New railway station building with an avec shop, in timber construction to MINERGIE level, with pellet heating.

Direct commission
Status
Completed

Preliminary design to construction

The new SBB office in Bonstetten was built in timber right next to and parallel with the tracks. The building is a long, simple block with canopies attached along the passenger area.

The brief comprises the SBB premises with ticket hall, offices, a staff room and storage. Separated by a toilet area for customers and staff, the other half of the building is used by an avec shop with its own storage and plant rooms.

The façades are characterised by large areas of glazing with floor-to-ceiling sliding doors, making the interior highly transparent. The closed façade elements are clad with rear-ventilated expanded metal, giving the building a character that is both technical and refined. In contrast to the exterior, which no longer reveals the timber construction, the interior surfaces consist mainly of raw, painted OSB panels.

The building was constructed to the MINERGIE standard and has wood pellet heating and ventilation with heat recovery.
