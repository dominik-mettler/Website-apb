Focus

School buildings in the Zurich Oberland and on Lake Zurich

apb architekten ag from Uster has completed 16 school and kindergarten buildings since 2002 – in Gossau, Grüningen, Grüt, Herrliberg, Hombrechtikon, Maur, Schwerzenbach, Uster and Wald. 8 of them after winning a competition or consultant selection (1st place).

Clients: City of Uster; Hombrechtikon School Board; Municipality of Gossau; Municipality of Grüningen; Municipality of Herrliberg; Municipality of Hombrechtikon; Municipality of Maur; Municipality of Wald; Nänikon-Greifensee School Board; Schwerzenbach Primary School Board; Uster Primary School; Uster Secondary School Board.
