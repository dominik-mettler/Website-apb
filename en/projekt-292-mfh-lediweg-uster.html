Lediweg Terraced Building Uster

New terraced building with exclusive owner-occupied apartments

New terraced building with seven exclusive owner-occupied apartments to MINERGIE level, in an exceptional location with views in Uster-Wermatswil.

The new “Blickpunkt Lediweg” development is located on the sunny hill of Wermatswil, on the north-eastern edge of Uster. Surrounded by detached houses and apartment buildings, the terraced building blends into its surroundings and nestles into the slope. The seven owner-occupied apartments, all facing south-west, are accessed from Hanfroosenweg and via an underground car park with 15 spaces.

On four residential levels, the garden, ground and upper floors each contain two generous, individual 3.5- to 5.5-room apartments with living areas of 130 m² to 170 m², each with a terrace opening directly onto the garden or a roof terrace. The attic floor is reserved for a single apartment of 190 m² with roof terraces totalling 150 m².

The apartments are reached via a staircase and lift on the hillside within the building. The main entrance is at street level from Hanfroosenweg on the ground floor. The very deep building is structured by two light wells, which create accents of light in the entrance and living areas of the apartments and in the common circulation areas. Visible incisions in the west façade also create zones and separations between the individual apartments.

Staggered and stepped towards the south-west, the building appears as a solid block that opens up transparently to the sunny side with large areas of glass. Two window types define its monolithic appearance: large, floor-to-ceiling windows opening directly onto the roof terrace, and narrow, horizontal windows lighting and ventilating the ancillary rooms.

Projecting roofs allow covered terraces to alternate with open roof terraces. On the garden and ground floors, the slope means that the apartments open directly onto the outdoor space.
