Flamingo Children’s Hospice Fällanden

New children’s hospice as a care facility

New building for Switzerland’s first children’s hospice, for children with life-limiting illnesses, close to Lake Greifensee.

Procurement
Direct commission
Status
Completed
Completion
October 2025
Scope of services
SIA stages 2.1 and 3.1 to 5.3

approx. 10,000 m³ building volume (SIA 416)

Situation

The site lies right next to the Roman Catholic parish centre of St. Catherine of Siena in Fällanden, the Sunnetal retirement centre and Lätten Primary School, and was acquired under a building lease. It is part of the public zone and, adjoining a reserve zone, occupies an exceptionally attractive position on the edge of Fällanden. Close by are the Zwicky site, the village centre with Fällanden’s community centre, and Lake Greifensee.

Brief

The Swiss Children’s Hospice Foundation (Stiftung Kinderhospiz Schweiz) is building a children’s hospice to provide professional support for children and young people with life-limiting illnesses and their families. The ground floor houses eight care rooms, a nurses’ station, a care bathroom and storerooms, as well as the catering area with a kitchen and a lounge and dining room. The first floor contains six guest rooms and two connectable rooms, a lounge area with a terrace, and a homework and play room. The second floor accommodates offices and meeting rooms, as well as a multi-purpose room for 50 people.

Appearance

The three-storey building is a subtractive volume with incisions for two roof terraces. The individual windows are slightly offset from floor to floor. The incisions respond to the neighbouring buildings and show consideration for them.

Construction

The building is of conventional construction, with a basement, lift shaft and floor slabs in concrete. The external walls are of concrete and masonry with an external thermal insulation composite system. To allow for future changes of use, the non-load-bearing internal walls remain flexible and can be removed.

Materials

The thermal insulation composite system is finished with a mineral render. Around the wood-metal windows, rendered panels stand out from the rest of the render in texture and colour.

Energy and building services

Geothermal probes are drilled on the site, and a heat pump supplies the heating circuit and hot water. Controlled ventilation is divided between three units. The building has wheelchair-accessible bathrooms, a care bathroom and a photovoltaic system with battery storage for power cuts.
